    What runs on its own

    1. Every hourThe Today board at the top of this Hub refreshes for everyone: shipping clock, customer issues, production, content, time off and supplies (6:27am–5:27pm, Mon–Sat).
    2. Every hourOpen Shopify orders load into the Pack Station queue and shipped orders are logged for the On time tab (6:51am–4:51pm, Mon–Sat). At 7:51am and 12:51pm Amber gets a Slack nudge if any order is past 24 hours; Stephanie hears at noon if any are past 48.
    3. Every hourReleased batches, shelf counts and ritual stock sync to Shopify inventory (6:53am–5:53pm, Mon–Sat).
    4. Every hourNew time-off requests go to Stephanie on Slack; approved time off is announced in #all-rr-collective-daily-sync and shown on everyone’s Team Station. New customer issues go to whoever is handling them, an urgent one nobody has picked up in 3 hours goes to Hollie, and Hollie gets a nudge (never the text) when Stephanie sends a private note (7:07am–6:07pm).
    5. Every hourAnything Hollie sends from the Creative Studio lands in Nate’s Ads Station, and Nate’s replies come back (7:41am–7:41pm).
    6. Every 2 hoursThe Owner Snapshot and the Ads Station scale gate refresh from Shopify, Meta and every station; guardrail flags go to Nate automatically (7:19am–7:19pm).
    7. Mon 6:29amThe Growth Lead writes the weekly growth brief, updates the scorecard, adds recommendations and answers your questions; Hollie gets a Slack heads-up. First Monday of each quarter it reviews the whole roadmap.
    8. LiveQ4 sales at the top of this page update from Shopify every few minutes while you have it open (Hollie and Nate only).
    9. Mon 6:41amThe Q4 forecast on the Inventory Station refreshes from the last 30 days of sales (through Dec 31).
    10. 3rd of the monthNate gets a reminder in his Ads Station and on Slack to pull last month’s P&L from Finaloop and share it with Hollie by the 10th.
    11. 6:53amDaily inventory check: anything under 20 units is emailed and pushed to Hollie.
    12. 6:58amYesterday’s sales report, by traffic source and new vs returning, goes to Hollie.
    13. 7:14amLow-stock products are added to Joella’s production schedule; Stephanie gets a Slack list (weekdays).
    14. 2:56pmLow shipping supplies, ingredients and packaging go to Stephanie on Slack (Mon–Sat).
    15. 9:47pmEach packer’s shipping accuracy is copied into their Team Station report (nightly).
