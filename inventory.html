      Rituals

      Rituals come out of the jars inside them when they’re packed. Their Shopify stock is set to however many full sets the jars can make. (The Best Sellers boxes are pre-made, so they’re listed above instead.)

      Product costs (COGS)

      Cost to make one unit: ingredients (each recipe amount × what you paid, divided by units per batch) plus packaging per unit. Material prices are set on the Materials tab. A “+” means some prices or amounts are still missing, so the real cost is higher. Only Hollie and Stephanie see costs.
